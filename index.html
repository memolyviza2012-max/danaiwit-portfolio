About Me

I began my creative journey by earning a Bachelor of Science in Animation from Chiang Mai University in 2015. Over more than a decade of hands-on production experience, I have developed deep mastery as a Senior 3D Modeler & Texture Artist, specializing as a Blender expert in precision Hard-Surface 3D modeling. My specialized modeling scope spans architectural structures, industrial machinery, FPV racing drones, high-precision surgical medical equipment, and complex mechanical assemblies.

Beyond creative 3D artistry, I manage corporate IT Support & Infrastructure Operations and operate as an Advanced AI Integrator & Power User. I integrate 3D production pipelines and IT systems with cutting-edge Generative AI to build automated workflows across web development, system analysis, technical copywriting, automated Thai game localization, and award-winning AI filmmaking.

As the studio's Lead Cameraman & Photographer, I have documented company milestones and culture since its inception. My end-to-end media skillset covers professional filming (operating gimbals like DJI Ronin RS5), photo retouching, and video editing.
