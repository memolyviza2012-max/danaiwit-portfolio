Modular Environment Kits

Two stylized Blender environments built around different settlement layouts. Each personal portfolio kit contains 28 reusable assets.

River trading town

Alderlock Crossing ↓

A toll bridge feeds streets that run parallel to the river. Stepped gables mark the merchant rows, with warehouses, cranes and cargo boats along the quay.

Woodland settlement

Bramblewatch Sanctuary ↓

Curving paths connect forest clearings. Acorn-shaped cottages, two-level tree homes and circular gardens distinguish the small building clusters.

Site plans compared

Alderlock follows a linear quay. Bramblewatch branches into woodland clearings.

Alderlock Crossing

The quay links the bridge to timber landings and cargo yards. Rowhouses frame the market and guild square, while loading hatches and crane jibs make the waterfront’s function readable at a distance.

Project roles and validation

Danaiwit Kanthawong defined the creative brief and directed both projects. Codex assisted with design, created geometry using Blender Python, and handled materials, scene assembly, lighting, and rendering.

The site plans are based on Blender scene placements. Evaluated triangle counts were checked against all 56 GLB exports. Unity import and gameplay have yet to be tested.
